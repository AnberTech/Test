About Silver Travertine

Square and rectangular tiles, laid in stack bond, running bond, ⅓ offset or herringbone on floors and walls.

Honed: Ground to a smooth, silky matte finish without polishing. Its non-reflective, contemporary look makes it a frequent choice for interior floors.

You need about 41.1 tiles of this product per m² (with 2 mm joints). Allow about 46 tiles with 10% waste; final quantities are confirmed when quoting.

KARABEL

Karabel Natural Stone is a marble and travertine manufacturer founded in 2014 in the Denizli Organised Industrial Zone (Honaz, Türkiye). It produces travertine and marble mosaics, tiles, Versailles (mini set) patterns, hexagons and split-face wall panels, cuts to size, and sells in Türkiye and abroad.
