About Yellow Travertine

Wall cladding panels of split-face strips laid in steps. Z-shaped ends let panels interlock for a continuous rock texture on interior and exterior walls.

Split-face: The stone is mechanically split to expose a rugged, rock-like relief. Its play of light and shadow adds depth to fireplaces, facades, garden and feature walls.

You need about 27.1 sheets of this product per m² (with 2 mm joints). Allow about 30 sheets with 10% waste; final quantities are confirmed when quoting.

KARABEL

Karabel Natural Stone is a marble and travertine manufacturer founded in 2014 in the Denizli Organised Industrial Zone (Honaz, Türkiye). It produces travertine and marble mosaics, tiles, Versailles (mini set) patterns, hexagons and split-face wall panels, cuts to size, and sells in Türkiye and abroad.
