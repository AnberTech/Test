About Toros Black Marble

Small pieces mounted on roughly 30×30 cm mesh sheets. Sheets interlock for fast, even installation on shower floors, backsplashes and curved surfaces.

Polished: Polished to a mirror gloss that shows veining and colour contrast at their most vivid. An elegant finish for interior walls and floors.

You need about 10.9 sheets of this product per m² (with 2 mm joints). Allow about 12 sheets with 10% waste; final quantities are confirmed when quoting.

KARABEL

Karabel Natural Stone is a marble and travertine manufacturer founded in 2014 in the Denizli Organised Industrial Zone (Honaz, Türkiye). It produces travertine and marble mosaics, tiles, Versailles (mini set) patterns, hexagons and split-face wall panels, cuts to size, and sells in Türkiye and abroad.
