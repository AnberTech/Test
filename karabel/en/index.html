Finish guide

Tumbled
Pieces are drum-tumbled to soften and round the edges and give the surface an aged, antique texture. It diffuses light without glare and suits patios, pool surrounds, gardens and rustic interiors.
Polished
Polished to a mirror gloss that shows veining and colour contrast at their most vivid. An elegant finish for interior walls and floors.
Honed
Ground to a smooth, silky matte finish without polishing. Its non-reflective, contemporary look makes it a frequent choice for interior floors.
Split-face
The stone is mechanically split to expose a rugged, rock-like relief. Its play of light and shadow adds depth to fireplaces, facades, garden and feature walls.
Filled and polished
Travertine pores are filled and the surface is polished to a high shine — smooth, glossy and easy to wipe clean.
Filled and honed
Travertine's natural pores are filled, then the surface is honed smooth and matte. With no open pores to collect dirt, it is easy to clean.
Filled and tumbled
Pore-filled travertine that is then tumbled for rounded edges and an antique look — the warmth of tumbled stone with a more closed surface.
Unpolished
Left unpolished after cutting, keeping the stone's natural matte texture.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Karabel Natural Stone and what does it make?

Karabel Natural Stone is a marble and travertine manufacturer operating since 2014 in the Denizli Organised Industrial Zone (Honaz, Denizli, Türkiye). It produces travertine and marble mosaics, tiles, Versailles (mini set) patterns, hexagons and split-face wall panels, and cuts to size.

What is the difference between travertine and marble?

Travertine is a naturally porous stone formed by calcium carbonate deposited by mineral springs, known for warm cream, beige, grey and brown tones. Marble forms when limestone is transformed by heat and pressure; it is denser and shows distinct veining. Denizli is Türkiye's best-known travertine region.

What is the difference between honed, tumbled and polished?

Honed stone is ground smooth and matte without polishing. Tumbled pieces are turned in a drum so the edges round off and the surface gains an antique texture. Polished stone is buffed to a mirror gloss that shows the veining most vividly.

What is filled travertine?

Filled travertine has its natural pores closed with a filler. The surface looks more closed and even, does not trap dirt and is easy to clean. Unfilled travertine keeps its natural porous texture.

How many mosaic sheets do I need per square metre?

With mosaic sheets of about 30×30 cm you need on average 10.9 sheets per m² (with 2 mm joints, before waste). Allow about 12 sheets with 10% waste. Each product page shows the value calculated from that product's own sheet size.

What is the difference between first grade, second grade and export grade?

In natural stone, grades describe how strict the selection is — sorted by colour consistency, pores and surface defects. Export is the most selective grade. The best decision for a project is made from a sample.
