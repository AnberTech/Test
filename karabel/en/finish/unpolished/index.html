KARABEL

Karabel Natural Stone is a marble and travertine manufacturer founded in 2014 in the Denizli Organised Industrial Zone (Honaz, Türkiye). It produces travertine and marble mosaics, tiles, Versailles (mini set) patterns, hexagons and split-face wall panels, cuts to size, and sells in Türkiye and abroad.
